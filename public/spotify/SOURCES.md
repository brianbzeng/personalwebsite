# Spotify branding

Unmodified official SVGs, downloaded September 5, 2026.

- `icon-white.svg`: `Primary_Logo_White_RGB.svg` from https://developer.spotify.com/images/guidelines/design/2024-spotify-logo-icon.zip
- `icon-primary.svg`: `Primary_Logo_Green_RGB.svg` from the same official icon bundle; displayed in grayscale in the Dock to match the desktop theme.

The full logo remains visible in the widget footer while the upper-right slot shows decorative bars during playback and nothing while idle.
Album and playlist artwork comes directly from Spotify, without grayscale, overlays or cropping.
